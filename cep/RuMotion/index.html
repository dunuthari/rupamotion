Ru Motion

Version 1.0
Motion toolkit for After Effects

How it works

1   Select a layer or layers in your timeline.

2   Click an effect in the panel. It starts at the playhead.

3   Retime the keyframes, or click again to replace it.

What's inside

•  Fades, slides and pops

•  Text effects and typewriters

•  Shapes, camera and easing

Good to know

•  Undo with Ctrl / Cmd + Z

•  Layers are named “Ru …”

•  Cut tools sit at the bottom

Ru Motion

Version 1.0
Motion toolkit for After Effects

How it works

  1. 1
    Select a layer or layers in your timeline.
  2. 2
    Click an effect in the panel. It starts at the playhead.
  3. 3
    Adjust timing by moving the keyframes, or press the button again to replace it.

What's inside

  • Fades, slides and pops
  • Text effects and typewriters
  • Shapes, camera and easing

Good to know

  • Undo with Ctrl / Cmd + Z
  • Layers are named “MK …”
  • Cut tools sit at the bottom
